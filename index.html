💛 Meet your child's caregiver

Meet Ale, a caregiver with a nurse's heart

Hello, my name is Ale, and I am the founder of Ale's Home Childcare. I am originally from Mexico, and caring for children has always been a special part of my life. With a background in nursing, early childhood education, and years of experience as a nanny, babysitter, and teacher, I have dedicated myself to creating a safe, loving environment where children feel valued and cared for.

As a parent, leaving your child in someone else's care is a big decision, and I understand the trust families place in me. My goal is to provide more than childcare. I want to create a second home where children feel loved, respected, and supported every day.

At Ale's Home Childcare, I believe children learn best when they feel safe, happy, and encouraged. Through play, creativity, routines, and positive guidance, I help children build confidence, develop social skills, become more independent, and enjoy every step of their early years.

I love watching children discover new things, reach milestones, and grow into their own unique personalities. I am honored to support families by providing a nurturing place where children can learn, laugh, explore, and feel at home.

As a bilingual provider in English and Spanish, I look forward to building a strong relationship with each family and being part of your child's early journey.

Helpful details before you reach out

Quick answers about Ale's licensed bilingual in-home daycare in Fremont, CA.

Is Ale's Home Childcare licensed?

Yes. Ale's Home Childcare is a licensed childcare program in Fremont, California. The California license number shown on the site is 015701501.

Do children learn in English and Spanish?

Yes. Children hear and practice both English and Spanish through songs, calendar time, colors, shapes, numbers, letters, emotions, art, and daily routines.

What are the childcare hours?

Ale's Home Childcare is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM.

Are meals included?

Yes. Breakfast, a snack, and lunch are included each day, so families do not need to pack a lunchbox.

Where is the daycare located?

Ale's Home Childcare is an in-home daycare in Fremont, CA, serving local Fremont families and nearby neighborhoods when the location is a good fit.

How can parents schedule a tour or ask about openings?

Parents can call or text (206) 609-1653, email alehomegroup@gmail.com, or use the contact section on the website to ask questions, schedule a visit, or check current openings.
