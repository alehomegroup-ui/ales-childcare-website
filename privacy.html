Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Ale's Home Childcare respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry, contact us, or interact with our ads.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an inquiry form, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, if provided
  • Your child's age or childcare needs, if included in your message
  • How you found us, if selected
  • Any other details you share in your message

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, answer questions, discuss childcare openings, schedule visits, and communicate with families about Ale's Home Childcare.

Website Analytics and Advertising

Our website may use analytics and advertising tools, including Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, Ahrefs Web Analytics, and Meta Pixel. These tools may collect information such as pages visited, clicks, device/browser information, approximate location, and interactions with our website or ads.

We use this information to understand website performance, improve the website, measure advertising results, and show more relevant ads. These third-party providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the website, process inquiry forms, measure website activity, or manage advertising. We may also share information if required by law.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website. Parents or guardians may choose to share childcare-related details when contacting us.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to families, manage childcare communication, and maintain business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your Choices

You can contact us to ask about information you provided, request corrections, or ask us to delete your inquiry information when possible. You can also adjust browser settings to limit cookies or tracking technologies.
